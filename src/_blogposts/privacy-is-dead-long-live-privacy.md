> This was originally supposed to be a part of my other post about [my love for OpenStreetMap](/blog/), but it ended up being too.. negative (?) which is why I decided to split it into a separate post.

I *hate* surveillance cameras. I hate how governments, police, and even private entities watching and recording everything has seemingly become the global norm. I hate how I can't do basically *anything* online without my data being shared with 600 different entities, and Google knowing the name of my firstborn before I've even considered having a child.

Additionally, with corporations like [Flock Safety](https://www.flocksafety.com/) putting up [cameras on every other corner](https://maps.deflock.org/) and [Palantir](https://www.palantir.com/) spying on everyone, the real world is more surveilled than ever before as well. This means I can neither go outside nor stay inside on my PC without being recorded in some way.

I don't think this should be the norm. In fact, I think this should be the exact opposite of the norm. I think everyone should be *completely* against this kind of activity.

The unfortunate part, from how I understand it, is that most "average" people don't have any clue how bad it has gotten. It feels a lot like everyone is just accepting it "because it makes it safer". Whenever I suggest people around me don't share passwords or I recommend encryption for things I get told "well I have nothing to hide" or "what are you, a criminal?" which makes no sense at all because everyone has something to hide.

Now, while I don't know how much I can do about the digital privacy issues myself, nor how to inform people about the reality of these issues, I *do* know how to inform people of the amount of cameras everywhere. And with this I loop back to OpenStreetMap from above.

## Surveillance under Surveillance

MapComplete has an *amazing* page called [Surveillance under Surveillance](https://mapcomplete.org/surveillance), which filters its view down to a few essential components:

- Surveillance cameras
- ALPR cameras
- Speed cameras
- Police offices, checkpoints, detention centers, and related.

For me, the most important here are the first three. These allow me to easily add cameras as I spot them outside, and add them to OSM. The page also then allows me to get an overview of where the cameras around me are, and lets me easily show others how bad the surveillance situation really has gotten.
